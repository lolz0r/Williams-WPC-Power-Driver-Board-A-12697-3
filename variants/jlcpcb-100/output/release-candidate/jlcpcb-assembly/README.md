# JLCPCB mixed SMT and through-hole assembly upload

Upload **`jlcpcb-assembly/BOM.csv` and `jlcpcb-assembly/CPL.csv` together**, with the existing `gerbers-and-drills.zip`. Both CSVs describe one board; select 100 boards in the order. Do not multiply the placement entries by 100 or include procurement spares as placements.

The pair contains all **440 purchased electronic pieces per board**: 345 SMT parts, 69 other through-hole parts, 13 additional through-hole fuse holders, and 13 cartridges inserted into those holders (the 69 includes the two empty spare holders). More simply, there are **345 SMT entries and 95 through-hole/inserted-part entries**. These correspond to 426 native electrical references, expanded for the separate holders and two J115 headers. The BOM contains 69 catalog part groups, with exactly the same expanded references as the CPL.

The old `all-electronics-positions.csv` used engineering-report headers, which were unsuitable for direct CPL upload. That filename is now also a copy of the corrected combined CPL. Its matching upload BOM is `jlcpcb-assembly/BOM.csv`. The detailed engineering table is now `assembly/electronics-placement-details.csv`. `bom/all-components.csv` remains the complete human-readable assembly/procurement record, including external hardware and unpopulated board features.

The CPL uses JLCPCB's five columns: `Designator,Mid X,Mid Y,Layer,Rotation`. Coordinates are numeric millimetres at the unchanged absolute Gerber origin, with Cartesian Y positive upwards; negative Y values are intentional. Rotation is counterclockwise and the populated side is `Top`. SMT XY coordinates preserve native body origins; part-specific rotations are corrected against the purchased library. `native-smt-positions.csv` is a raw engineering record, not an upload file. Through-hole positions use component body centers, including the offset between a TO-220 body and its pins and the dimensions of the prepared TE header housings.

## Manual operations required for complete assembly

Copy **`jlcpcb-assembly/PCBA-remark.txt`** into the PCBA order remarks and provide **`jlcpcb-assembly/placement-map.csv`** as the assembly reference. JLCPCB supports mixed SMT/THT assembly, but part matching and acceptance of these particular manual operations must be checked in the quotation. For the requested 100-board order, use Standard PCBA; JLCPCB currently lists Economic PCBA up to 50 boards.

- **Fuses:** F103 through F111 and F113 through F116 identify the 13 cartridges. FH103 through FH111 and FH113 through FH116 identify their separately purchased holders. A holder and its cartridge share the corresponding native F-reference footprint. JLCPCB documents a greater-than-0.2 mm CPL separation to select stacked components; each cartridge has **+0.3 mm in upload X only**. The actual physical cartridge center is the holder center, recorded in `placement-map.csv`. Instruct the assembler to insert the cartridge into the holder and install its included cover. F101/F102 are empty spare holders; F112 is the soldered TA7 fuse.
- **J115:** J115A and J115B are separately purchased six-position headers at their individual body centers. They occupy native J115 pins 1–6 and 7–12. Remove local post 3 of J115B for native key pin 9. Do not buy or place a third J115 header.
- **Prepared headers:** J120/J121 require trimming from 13 to 11 positions; J133/J134/J135 from 10 to 9. All header keys must follow `assembly/connector-keys.csv`. The native PCB retains the holes at removed key posts.
- **BR3 and hardware:** BR3 requires the documented lead forming and mounting stack. Heatsinks, fasteners and thermal paste have no qualified JLC stock codes in this design. They are listed in `external-hardware.csv` for quotation/consignment and fitting. The two upload CSVs contain all electronics; accepting them alone does not confirm complete mechanical assembly.

The local verifier checks exact CSV headers, finite coordinates, BOM/CPL equality, all purchased quantities, native coverage, matching SMT-only/mixed files, every fuse/holder stack, THT housing centers, and a documented library audit for all 69 part codes. It independently compares functional pads, tests J113's absolute holes, checks inward friction-wall directions, and explicitly reports missing/inconsistent supplier data. The corrected native connector models are mechanically rechecked and rendered. Live JLCPCB import, preview and assembly acceptance remain untested here; the manual gates above require resolution before manufacture.
