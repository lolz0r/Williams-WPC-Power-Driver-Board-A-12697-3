# Fabrication and assembly notes - WPC Power Driver replacement, cost-optimised board

Deliverables for the fab/assembler are in `output/` (regenerated by `bash tools/export.sh` from the routed board):
`wpc_power_driver_cost-gerbers.zip` (RS-274X Gerbers with Protel extensions + `.gbrjob`), `drill/` (Excellon, plated and non-plated,
plus drill maps), `assembly/` (position files, IPC-D-356 net-list `wpc_power_driver_cost.d356`, assembly drawing, per-layer PDFs),
`bom/` (grouped and flat BOM CSV; `BOM-cost.md` with manufacturer, MPN, distributor link and prices), `reports/` (ERC, DRC).

## Assembly

* All 375 placed components are on the **top side** (`assembly/wpc_power_driver_cost-pos.csv`; DNP parts are listed separately in
  `-pos-all-incl-dnp.csv`).  SMT reflow first (0805/1210/2512 passives, SMA/SMC/SOD-123 diodes, DPAK MOSFETs, SOIC logic, HSOP-8 bucks,
  SRP inductors, SOT-23 transistors, SMD LEDs), then through-hole: Molex KK connectors, the 2x17 box header J113, Keystone 3517 fuse clips
  (32 clips = 16 fuse positions), the five bridge rectifiers, the TO-220 triacs, the snap-in / radial electrolytics, the through-hole
  test-point pads.  Selective or wave soldering is fine; the 4.9 mm PowerPAD via arrays are on the top side only.
* **Connector key pins**: several harness plugs are keyed by a plugged position; the corresponding header pin must be omitted (or cut) so
  the original harness mates in the original orientation.  The list is in `docs/ASSEMBLY_NOTES.md` (connector key pins), derived from the
  STTNG manual's connector tables.
* **J113 orientation**: pin 1 of the 34-way box header is at the position shown on the assembly drawing (polarising notch as on the
  original board); the ribbon to CPU J211 is 1:1.
* **Schottky bridge diodes D101-D116** (D2PAK): the tab is the cathode and sits on a 2 oz heat-spreading pour with a 3 x 3 via array
  (tools/rail_pours.py); reflow with the tab fully wetted - the pours are the heatsink (no separate part).
* **Heatsinks**: the five G.I. triacs (Q6-Q10) and the bridge rectifiers need the heatsinks listed in `docs/THERMAL_AND_PROTECTION.md`
  and the BOM (thermal pad or paste, M3 hardware, torque 0.5-0.6 Nm for TO-220, 1.0 Nm for the GBJ/GBU tabs).  The original board
  bolts its bridge blocks to the chassis bracket; this board carries its own.
* **Fuses** are 5 x 20 mm (Littelfuse 239 Slo-Blo / 217 fast, ratings printed on the silkscreen next to each position); F101/F102 get
  clips only (no fuse) on Fliptronic games.  Ship with fuses fitted.
* **DNP**: the relay option K1 and its parts (marked DNP in the BOM), R198.  Do not populate.
* **Polarity**: the electrolytic capacitors' negative stripe follows the silkscreen mark; bridge rectifiers: "+" lead per the silkscreen;
  LEDs: cathode mark.
* **Conformal coating**: none (matches the original).  Clean no-clean flux residue around the fuse clips.

## Test before shipping

1. Visual: fuse clip alignment (a 5 x 20 mm fuse must seat in every position), key pins removed, heatsinks torqued, no bent DPAK tabs.
2. Power-up without the machine: apply the secondaries through the original fuses or a bench transformer set; check the rails at the test
   points: TP2 +5.0 V (+/- 2 %), TP1 +12 V power (unregulated, 10-16 V), +12 V digital (12.0 V) on J114-1/2, TP8 +18 V (~14-18 V unloaded),
   TP7 +20 V, TP6 "+50 V" (66-78 V DC).  Verify ZERO CROSS at J113-34 is a 5 V, 60 Hz square wave.
3. With BLANKING (J113-31) left open (pulled high on the board) no solenoid, flasher, lamp column or triac output may be active.
4. In the machine: run the STTNG solenoid, lamp and G.I. tests from the diagnostics menu.
